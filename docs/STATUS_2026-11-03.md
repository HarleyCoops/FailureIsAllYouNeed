## Next experiment, when resumed

First define the behavioral correction, preservation gate, and a new final audit.
Then compare constrained analytic edits with a small failure-focused training
baseline under the same budget. Rejection is a valid outcome. Neither the failure
of this sweep nor a successful future training run alone proves a capacity limit.

The original inspiration remains
[Sparks of Weight-Space Programming](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/).
This work is an independent conceptual replication and extension with different
policies, simulators, tools, and evaluation protocols.
